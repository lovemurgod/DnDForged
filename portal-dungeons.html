Tactical Map Technology

Portal-Linked Mega-Dungeons & Interconnected Maps

Build sprawling multi-floor complexes, winding cavern networks, and planar rifts across modular maps—without browser crashes or performance lag.

The Challenge: Colossal Single-Image Battlemaps

Architecture

Running ambitious campaigns often forces Dungeon Masters to choose between cramped combat maps or massive 10,000-pixel graphics that choke browser memory and stutter during player movement.

Single-Map Limitations

Loading an entire megadungeon in one file forces every player's browser to decode hundreds of megabytes of raw textures at once. Mobile phones and older laptops frequently crash or drop to single-digit framerates.

The ForgeDVTT Solution

Split your adventure into fast, modular tactical maps connected by geometric portals. Players explore seamlessly while the application loads only the active environment, guaranteeing buttery-smooth 60 FPS tactical play.

Core Portal Capabilities

Engine

Non-Euclidean Space

Portals support arbitrary facing angles (0° to 360°), directional flips, and one-way secret passages. Connect spiral stairways, vertical elevator shafts, and impossible teleportation traps.

Distance-Bending Ruler

Measurement lines don’t break at thresholds. Drag your tactical ruler toward a portal, and it bends across the boundary into the target map, calculating continuous character movement.

Lightweight Memory Footprint

Only the map containing active player tokens is streamed to connecting browsers. Distant floors and unvisited dungeon wings remain stored safely on disk until entered.

How Dungeon Masters Configure Portals

Workflow
  1. 1

    Select the Portal Geometry Tool

    In the Game Master map editor, click the Portal icon on the left canvas toolbar. Choose between line-segment doors or radial teleportation circles.

  2. 2

    Draw the Entrance & Set Orientation

    Draw the threshold along a doorway or archway. Set the compass facing angle so tokens entering from the south arrive in the correct orientation.

  3. 3

    Link to Destination Map & Target Portal

    Select the target map from your campaign library and assign the corresponding arrival portal. Two-way linkages update automatically.

Technical Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Can players see through portals before stepping through?

Portals focus on token transition and movement measurement. Line-of-sight and fog of war remain contained to each map until a token crosses the threshold, keeping secret rooms completely hidden from unauthorized vision.

Do portals work with hex grids as well as square grids?

Yes. ForgeDVTT's coordinate mapping engine calculates distance and vector entry angles across standard square tactical grids, point-to-point measurements, and hexagonal maps.

Can I lock a portal to prevent players from triggering it prematurely?

Yes. The Game Master can toggle any portal between active and locked states directly from the canvas interface, preventing accidental transitions during exploration.
