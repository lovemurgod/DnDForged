🚀 How to Host as a DM

To host a campaign server on your own computer:

  1. Double-click start-online.bat on your PC.
  2. Paste your Cloudflare Tunnel Token when prompted (or press Enter for local play).
  3. Enter your subdomain slug (e.g. julz).
  4. Share your game link https://your-name.forgedvtt.com with your players!
