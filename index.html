Game Master Setup & Hosting Walkthrough

Host your private campaigns directly on your computer with complete data sovereignty.

1

Launch ForgeD VTT Desktop App or CLI

Download the Windows Desktop Setup installer (or run node launcher.js in terminal). The application automatically spins up your local WebSocket server and connects a secure Cloudflare Tunnel.

2

Choose Your Campaign Subdomain

When prompted, enter a unique subdomain slug for your campaign (e.g. curseofstrahd or tombofannihilation). This provides an isolated world directory for your maps, tokens, and chat history.

3

Share URL with Players

Send your players your session link (e.g. https://curseofstrahd.forgedvtt.com). They can connect instantly from any modern web browser or mobile tablet without installing software or registering accounts.

GM Pro Tip: You can run ForgeD VTT completely offline on a local LAN! Just connect players to your local IP address (e.g. http://192.168.1.100:3000).

Player Quickstart Guide

Joining a campaign session, moving tokens, and rolling dice.

1

Join Campaign via Subdomain

Enter your GM's campaign subdomain in the Sessions tab above or open the direct campaign link provided by your GM.

2

Set Your Character Name & Token

On the tabletop canvas, click your player avatar in the bottom toolbar to assign your character name, color outline, and token portrait.

3

Moving & Waypointing

Click and drag your token across the map grid. Hold Space while dragging to measure exact movement distance in feet.

Tactical Maps & Token Controls

Mastering the battlemap canvas, grid snapping, and creature token management.

1

Upload Battlemaps (PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG)

As GM, open the Map Manager panel on the top-left to upload high-resolution maps. ForgeD VTT automatically caches map assets for instantaneous party loading.

2

Grid Alignment & Snapping

Use the grid tool to adjust grid square sizes (e.g. 50px, 70px, 100px) and align with your map's drawn artwork. Toggle snapping on/off with Shift.

3

Token Radial Menu & Conditions

Right-click any token on canvas to access the radial menu: set HP, adjust elevation (flying/swimming), apply conditions (Poisoned, Stunned, Blinded), or duplicate tokens.

Dynamic Fog of War & Vision Modes

Immersive dungeon exploration with real-time darkness occlusion.

1

Enable Fog Layer

Toggle Enable Fog of War in the GM Toolbar. The entire canvas shrouds in darkness for players until revealed.

2

Reveal & Hide Brushes

Use the circular or polygon reveal tool to unmask dungeon rooms as players explore. Hold Alt to re-shroud areas in darkness.

3

Token Vision Radii

Assign darkvision (60 ft / 120 ft) or torchlight (20 ft bright / 20 ft dim) directly to character tokens. The fog automatically parts around moving tokens in real time.

3D Physics Dice Box & Roll Commands

WebGL simulated 3D dice with synchronized physics and roll macros.

1

Chat Dice Commands

Type standard dice notation into the chat box:

/r 1d20+7                             # Standard attack roll
/r 2d20kh1+5                           # Advantage (Keep Highest 1)
/r 2d20kl1+3                           # Disadvantage (Keep Lowest 1)
/gmroll 1d20+9                         # Secret GM Roll (hidden from players)
/r 8d6                                 # Fireball damage
2

1-Click Compendium Rolling

Click any highlighted attack modifier (e.g. +8 to hit) or damage formula in monster statblocks to automatically throw 3D dice across the tabletop.

5e Compendium & Character Sheet Integration

Drag-and-drop bestiary tokens, instant spell lookups, and automated calculations.

1

Drag & Drop Monsters onto Canvas

Open the in-game Bestiary drawer, search for any creature (e.g. Adult Red Dragon), and drag its card directly onto the canvas to instantly spawn an active monster token with full statblocks attached.

2

Character Sheet Linking

Link character sheets to player tokens for real-time HP bar updates, condition synchronization, and spell slot expenditure tracking.

How to Host a ForgeD VTT Session

Game Masters can host campaigns directly from their computer with zero port-forwarding using Cloudflare Tunnels.

1

Download & Launch Desktop App

Download and run the 1-click Windows Desktop Setup installer:

Download ForgeD VTT Setup (.exe)

Or for Node.js / CLI self-hosting, run: node launcher.js

2

Set Campaign Subdomain

Choose your session's unique subdomain slug (e.g. julz or cosmic) when prompted by the launcher.

3

Invite Players

Share your unique campaign URL with your players (e.g. https://julz.forgedvtt.com) to jump straight into the VTT tabletop!
