How to Host a DnDForged Session

Game Masters can host campaigns directly from their computer with zero port-forwarding using Cloudflare Tunnels.

1

Run Local Launcher

Start the DnDForged server launcher script on your host machine:

node launcher.js
2

Set Campaign Subdomain

Choose your session's unique subdomain slug (e.g. julz or cosmic) when prompted by the launcher.

3

Invite Players

Share your unique campaign URL with your players (e.g. https://julz.forgedvtt.com) to jump straight into the VTT tabletop!
