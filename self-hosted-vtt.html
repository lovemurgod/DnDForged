Local Server Freedom

Self-Hosted Virtual Tabletop Without Port Forwarding

Retain complete ownership of your campaign files, enjoy unlimited local asset storage, and let players join through secure web links—with zero router configuration.

The Self-Hosted Advantage

Ownership

Cloud-hosted tabletop platforms lock dynamic lighting, asset uploads, and large campaigns behind monthly subscriptions or restrictive 100 MB storage quotas. Self-hosting restores total creative freedom.

Local Campaign Storage

Your maps, monster tokens, audio, and player character sheets live directly on your SSD. Backups are as simple as copying your local campaign folder—no subscription paywalls or platform lock-in.

Unlimited Asset Library

Store gigabytes of high-definition battlemaps, 4K WebM animations, and massive homebrew compendiums without watching a cloud storage meter or paying monthly server hosting fees.

How 1-Click Online Tunneling Works

Networking

Traditionally, self-hosting required logging into your home router, setting up static IP addresses, and forwarding TCP ports. ForgeDVTT eliminates this complexity through built-in Cloudflare quick tunnels.

1. Start Local Host

Click "Start Server" inside the ForgeDVTT Control Center. The local Node.js WebSocket engine spins up instantly on your machine.

2. Open Secure Tunnel

Click "Start Online Tunnel". The app establishes an outbound encrypted TLS tunnel to Cloudflare’s global edge network without opening router ports.

3. Share Player Link

Copy your unique, secure HTTPS link and send it to your group. Players open the link in any modern web browser and enter your campaign immediately.

Technical Details

Frequently Asked Questions

What internet upload speed does the Game Master need?

For standard games with static battlemaps, 3–5 Mbps of stable upload speed is sufficient for 4–6 players. If you stream multiple 4K WebM animated video maps simultaneously, a 10+ Mbps upload broadband connection is recommended.

Does my public IP address get exposed to players?

No. When using the built-in online tunnel, players connect to Cloudflare's secure edge network, which proxies the WebSocket traffic back to your local server. Your personal home IP address is never revealed to connecting browsers.

Can I run games entirely offline on a local area network (LAN)?

Yes. If you play in person with laptops or tablets connected to the same Wi-Fi router, players can connect directly using your computer’s local IP address (e.g. http://192.168.1.15:3000) without requiring an active internet connection.
