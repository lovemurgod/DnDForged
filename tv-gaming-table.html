In-Person & Hybrid Gaming

Virtual Tabletop for TV Gaming Tables & Digital Battlemats

Elevate in-person game nights with dedicated dual-display presentation. Run your campaign from your laptop while streaming a clean, synchronized battlemap to your TV table or projector.

The Challenge: Awkward In-Person Workarounds

Dual-Screen UX

Most virtual tabletops are designed exclusively for remote players on separate laptops. Running them at a physical table usually requires opening an incognito browser tab as a dummy player, which constantly breaks when the DM tries to prep.

Clunky Browser Workarounds

Managing two independent browser windows with separate logins results in constant alt-tabbing, accidental exposure of hidden monsters, and jerky camera movement whenever the DM pans to inspect distant areas.

Native Presentation Window

ForgeDVTT spawns a dedicated presentation display from the settings menu. It mirrors the active map, syncs party vision, and offers instant camera freeze controls so you can manage encounters without breaking immersion.

Core Presentation Mode Features

Capabilities

Zero DM Clutter

The player display hides all Game Master sidebars, creature statblocks, hidden layer tokens, and preparation notes, outputting a pure, high-resolution cinematic battlemap.

Camera Lock & Freeze

Lock camera mirroring to follow the active token, or hit Camera Freeze to lock the TV view in place while you pan across the map to stage upcoming traps or enemy reinforcements.

Party Vision Synchronization

Dynamic lighting, torchlight flicker, and fog of war calculate automatically from all allied party tokens, revealing rooms only as miniatures physically advance.

Digital Gaming Table Setup Tips

Hardware
  • Display Output: Connect your TV or projector as an extended desktop display (via HDMI, DisplayPort, or wireless casting). Drag the ForgeDVTT Presentation Window onto the screen and press F11 for fullscreen mode.
  • Physical Miniatures: If using physical resin or metal minis on a horizontal TV, place a 1/8-inch sheet of clear acrylic or tempered glass over the screen to guard against scratches.
  • Grid Calibration: Adjust the grid zoom inside ForgeDVTT so one tactical 5-foot square measures exactly 1 inch (25.4 mm) on your physical screen surface.

Technical Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I run an in-person TV table without an active internet connection?

Yes. The local ForgeDVTT server and Presentation Window run entirely offline on your Windows laptop. You do not need Wi-Fi or internet access to run local in-person sessions.

Does ForgeDVTT support 4K TV screens?

Yes. The canvas engine supports modern high-DPI displays and renders 4K static and animated WebM battlemaps crisply without pixelation.

Can remote players join while I use the TV table for in-person players?

Yes! This is ideal for hybrid campaigns. In-person players gather around the TV table viewing the Presentation Window, while remote party members connect via browser from home.
